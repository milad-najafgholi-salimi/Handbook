### What is a diagram?
#### Simple Definition

A **diagram** is a simplified, symbolic drawing that explains, shows relationships, or shows the structure of an idea, a process, or an object. It uses shapes, lines, and labels instead of realistic pictures.
### Common Examples You See Every Day

|Type of Diagram|What it Shows|Example Use|
|---|---|---|
|**Flowchart**|The steps in a process or a decision path.|Showing how to troubleshoot a printer problem.|
|**Venn Diagram**|How different sets of things overlap or are different.|Comparing the features of two products.|
|**Tree Diagram**|Hierarchical structure or branching possibilities.|A family tree or an organizational chart.|
|**Circuit Diagram**|How an electrical or electronic circuit works.|Showing wires, resistors, and chips inside a phone.|
|**Venn Diagram**|Overlapping relationships between groups.|Illustrating shared and unique traits of mammals and fish.|
